# moroccanorganicproducts.com - SEO Audit, Positioning and Page Plan

Checked: live site (old .php pages, via search results and homepage text) and the new local version (screenshots of /, /about-us, /products, /wholesale, /private-label-moroccan-beauty-products, /faq). October 2026.

## 1. Summary

This is the oldest of your sites ("since 2012", Marrakesh) and Google already knows its old .php URLs. That history is valuable - but only if the new version keeps it. Today the content is keyword-stuffed, makes claims that need proof, uses images with another brand's watermark, and targets exactly the same searches as your other sites. The new local version has a better design on the inner pages, but the homepage still uses the old template.

## 2. Critical Issues

### 2.1 Old URLs must redirect to the new ones (or you lose 14 years of history)
The live site uses .php URLs that are indexed: /about-us.php, /faq.php, /private-label-moroccan-beauty-products.php, /products/natural-beauty-of-moroccan-black-soap.php, and others. The new site uses /about-us, /faq, /private-label-moroccan-beauty-products, /products. Every old URL needs a permanent (301/308) redirect to its new equivalent. Without it, all old rankings and backlinks are lost on launch day.

### 2.2 Images with another brand's watermark, fake seals and stock/AI images
- The Aker Fassi image shows "Moroccan Organica" and a "100% quality guarantee" seal, plus "Buy in bulk" text burnt into the image.
- Stock or AI-looking images: the bearded man with a dropper, the 3D hair-follicle illustration, the prickly pear on white background.
Replace them with your own product photos. Images with another brand's name confuse buyers and link your sites together.

### 2.3 Keyword stuffing
The live homepage has lines written only for search engines, e.g. "Prickly Pear Seed Oil Opuntia Ficus Indica barbary fig seed oil wholesale", "Producer Moroccan argan oil suppliers Canada", "Moroccan argan oil beauty hair treatment serum cosmetic". Remove them all.

### 2.4 Claims that need proof (or must go)
- "Organic", "100% organic", "certified pure" - only if you hold a certificate (name the body, number and scope).
- "Industry leader", "leading in the manufacturing".
- "Anti Aging Moisturizer", "the most expensive oils in the world".
- "Beard growth", "nourish roots" - growth claims are medical-type claims.
- "Contains high levels of magnesium potassium" (ghassoul) - only with an analysis.
- Factual errors: the "argan oil for beard growth" product says it is made with "pure virgin castor oil"; "OEM" is written as "Other Equipment Manufacturer" (it is Original Equipment Manufacturer).

### 2.5 The brand name contains "Organic"
"Moroccan Organic Products" is your established brand and domain, so keep it. But because the name already says "organic", buyers and regulators will expect proof. Either show your organic certificates clearly (holder, body, number, scope), or make sure every product text says exactly what is certified and what is not.

### 2.6 One more site on the same keywords
This is the 6th site targeting "Moroccan beauty products wholesale", "private label Moroccan cosmetics" and "argan oil wholesale". The new Wholesale H1 ("Authentic Moroccan Organic Beauty Products Supplier") and Private Label H1 ("Private Label Moroccan Beauty Products Supplier") compete directly with moroccanbeautywholesale.com and moroccan-cosmetics.com. See section 4.

## 4. Positioning: Give This Site Its Own Job

Your sites today:

| Site | Role (agreed or proposed) |
|---|---|
| moroccan-cosmetics.com | Private label and brand building (guides, regulations) |
| moroccocosmetic.com | Marrakech factory and raw-material catalogue |
| moroccanbeautywholesale.com | Ready-to-sell products in small quantities for shops, hotels, online sellers |
| moroccanhammamproducts.com | Hammam and spa specialist |
| moroccanorganica.com | Main Organica Group brand |
| **moroccanorganicproducts.com** | **Proposed: the Moroccan oils specialist** |

**Proposed positioning:** "Argan oil and prickly pear seed oil producer since 2012 - bulk oils and oil-based private label (face oils, hair serums, beard oils) from 50 pieces."

Why: argan and prickly pear oil are the products this site already leads with ("Our Argan Oil and Prickly Pear Oil factory"); oils are a distinct buying decision (grades, COA, acidity, drums); and none of your other sites is an oils specialist. Black soap, ghassoul and other hammam products can stay in the catalogue as secondary products, linking to nothing outside this site.

**To avoid cannibalization:** remove "private label argan oil" as a target keyword from moroccan-cosmetics.com and let this site own it; moroccanbeautywholesale.com and moroccanhammamproducts.com should not target "argan oil wholesale/bulk".
