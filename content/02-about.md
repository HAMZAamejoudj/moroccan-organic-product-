## Why We Focus on Oils

Argan oil is easy to sell and easy to get wrong. In the years we have been supplying it, the same problems keep coming back from buyers who worked with other suppliers: oil diluted with cheaper oils, culinary oil sold as cosmetic, stock that had been stored too long, documents that did not match the batch.

Specialising in a small number of oils lets us control the things that matter: where the kernels come from, how and when the oil is pressed, how it is stored, and what goes into each document.
