## What to Check Before You Buy Argan Oil in Bulk

- **The grade.** Cosmetic oil comes from unroasted kernels and smells lightly nutty. A strong toasted smell means culinary oil.
- **The batch COA.** It should carry a batch number, an analysis date and the lab name.
- **A sample from the same batch.** Keep part of it sealed to compare with the delivery.
- **Packaging.** Food-grade containers, sealed, filled to limit air.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between cosmetic and culinary argan oil?
Cosmetic argan oil is pressed from raw kernels for skin and hair. Culinary argan oil is pressed from roasted kernels for food. They are not interchangeable.

### How should argan oil be stored?
Closed, full containers, away from light and heat. Oxidation starts with air, light and warmth.
