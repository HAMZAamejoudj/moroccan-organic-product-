# Culinary Argan Oil From Roasted Kernels

Culinary argan oil is pressed from lightly roasted argan kernels. It has a warm, nutty taste and is used raw: on salads, couscous, bread, or in amlou with almonds and honey.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can culinary argan oil be used on skin?
It can, but it smells strongly of roasted nuts. For cosmetics, use cosmetic argan oil.

### Can I cook with argan oil?
It is best used raw or added at the end of cooking, to keep its flavour.
