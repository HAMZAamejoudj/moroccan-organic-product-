## Why It Costs More Than Argan Oil

The seeds are small, hard and low in oil. Each litre needs [a very large weight of] fruit, harvested and processed by hand before pressing. A price far below the market usually means a diluted or mislabelled oil.

Yes, it is a common combination in private label face oils.
